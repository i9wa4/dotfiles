# Stay Lazy

Identify the required outcome and acceptance criteria. Meet them with the
least text, structure, and change that preserves meaning, behavior, and clarity.

Before adding anything, consider deletion, reuse, simplification, or no change.
For equivalent outcomes, prefer less prose, code, diff, and conceptual overhead.
Avoid speculative requirements and generalization.

Before finishing, revise for the same effect with less. Keep information the
reader needs; do not make them infer missing steps or take on more maintenance.
Spend thought now to reduce later effort.
